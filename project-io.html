Inside / Outside  ·  内部 / 外部

A service design proposal connecting Milan's distant neighborhoods with Fondazione Adolfo Pini through participatory art — bringing the institution outside and the community inside.

通过参与式艺术，连接米兰郊区社区与阿道夫·皮尼基金会——将机构带向街头，将社区带回艺术空间。

Year
2023
Studio

Team
Battiato · Deng · Garancini · Hirata · Kuerban
Type
Service Design · Community

IO aims to reach marginalised communities and engage middle-school kids through art activities at Fondazione Pini, Corso Garibaldi 2. The concept centres on exchanging ideas across cultures to build a more inclusive social environment — going beyond stereotypes and spreading the Foundation's vision across all of Milan.

Rooted in the cultural heritage of Adolfo Pini and Renzo Bongiovanni Radice, the project grew from a deep analysis of Brera and Milan's peripheries — where a significant gap in art education exists and many teenagers are disoriented, often facing complex family situations.

IO 旨在触达边缘化社区，为米兰初中生在 Fondazione Pini（Corso Garibaldi 2号）提供艺术活动。项目核心在于推动不同文化之间的思想交流，打造更具包容性的社会环境——突破偏见，向全体米兰市民传递基金会的愿景与使命。

项目根植于 Adolfo Pini 与 Renzo Bongiovanni Radice 的文化遗产，源于对布雷拉区与米兰近郊的深度调研——不同区域之间艺术教育差距显著，大量青少年感到迷失，家庭状况也往往较为复杂。

01

The Participatory Art

参与式艺术

Participatory art is an approach of making art which engages public participation in the creative process, letting them become co-authors, editors and observers of the work.

参与式艺术是一种让公众参与创作过程的艺术形式，使其成为作品的共同作者、编辑与观察者。

"This type of art is incomplete without viewers' physical interaction."

没有观众的身体参与，这类艺术便是不完整的。

What do the artists say? · 艺术家说

"Not like old-generation artists with a solitary mindset; young artists nowadays really like to work in groups. Lots of our projects actually involve mass participation, mostly with friends and other artists, but we are curious also about working with strangers like community residents."

与上一代独自创作的艺术家不同，当代年轻艺术家热衷于群体合作。我们的许多项目都涉及大规模参与——通常与朋友和其他艺术家合作，但我们也渴望与社区居民等陌生人共同创作。

— Winners of Pini Art Prize

CS 03

Artist-initiated Public Art

艺术家主导的公共艺术
PeterboroughCity Hall2022

The city of Peterborough adopted the Public Art Policy in 2009, recognizing the role public art plays in creating culturally vibrant cities. The city promotes the creation of contemporary artwork in the public area in collaboration with arts or community organizations or with local businesses.

彼得伯勒市于2009年制定《公共艺术政策》，认可公共艺术在打造文化活力城市中的价值，推动与艺术机构、社区组织及当地企业合作，在公共空间中创作当代艺术作品。

Target · 目标： Professional artists of all disciplines, communities and local businesses. / 各艺术门类的专业艺术家、社区及本地企业。
CS 04

Participatory Embroidery

参与式社区刺绣
AustraliaLiam Benson2013–2017

The Participatory Community Embroidery project collaborated with a local studio and community members from diverse ethnic backgrounds, to co-create the map and flag which are interpretations of Australian national identity.

参与式社区刺绣项目与当地工作室及来自不同族裔背景的社区成员合作，共同创作地图与旗帜，诠释澳大利亚的国家认同。

Target · 目标： Communities from different ethnic backgrounds. / 来自不同族裔背景的社区成员。

How does IO work with the current proposals?

IO如何在现有方案中发挥作用？

Fondazione Adolfo Pini wants to finance and help all the young artists. "Establish awards and scholarships for young artists" is the starting point of IO. IO targets every non-artist from different backgrounds, helping them work on their creativities together and discover each other, building new relationships inside and outside Fondazione Adolfo Pini.

皮尼基金会的使命是资助青年艺术家。IO以此为起点，将不同背景的非艺术家纳入目标群体，推动人们跨越边界共同激发创意，在基金会内外建立全新的人际连接与关系网络。

all together partecipating to the building of our core
共同参与，共同构建我们的核心

IO项目以皮尼基金会（Fondazione Pini）为核心枢纽，在现有方案的基础上支持青年艺术家；同时提出新方案，将非艺术家与艺术家混合群体纳入合作，通过新活动投入资源、建立连接，共同扩展至更多社区，形成更广泛的参与网络。

The Participatory Art

Participatory art projects, fueled by the vibrant diversity of artists from various backgrounds and nations, are breathing life into the city's streets. The hearth is the apecar, a symbol of mobility and connection. Thanks to the collaboration with local municipalities, these artistic caravans find their designated stops, unlocking creative spaces where communities come alive.

The call for IO permits artists to find people to create works of art and develop community projects, where the public becomes artist and the artist becomes public.

IO的召集令让艺术家得以找到参与者共同创作——公众化身艺术家，艺术家融入公众。以Apecar为核心，参与式艺术在城市街头生长。

Residence-Studio · 驻留工作室

Case Studies Matrix
& ReA! Kids

Case Studies Matrix · 案例定位矩阵

以"社区导向 vs. 艺术家导向"为纵轴、"盈利 vs. 非盈利"为横轴，将 PSArt、Nuba、Munson、MUBA、Art4all 等十余个机构映射在矩阵中，帮助团队明确 IO 在竞争格局中的差异化定位。

Case Study: ReA! Kids · 案例研究

ReA!（米兰）为非盈利机构，面向5–12岁儿童提供剧场表演课（Corso Teatro-Performance）与美术课（Corso di Arte），每课15欧元，月度套餐60–160欧元。服务持续进行中，合作对象涵盖学校与企业。

Res 12

MUBA & Munson:
Museum Learning

MUBA · 米兰儿童博物馆

面向4个月至11岁儿童，非盈利，通过企业赞助与学校合作提供课程，单次收费18–34欧元。

Munson · 纽约

盈利机构，提供展览、艺术节、夏令营等，服务从儿童延伸至成人，收费10–80美元，定价体系更多元。

Res 13

Art 3331 &
Milan PGT 2030

Art 3331 · 东京

已停办盈利机构，通过活动策划、社区菜园与驻留项目连接艺术家与公众，月费21–26万日元，是"艺术即日常"社区嵌入模式的有益参照。

Milan PGT 2030 · 城市总体规划

提出三项核心战略：连接近郊交通、工作机会促社会融合、公共空间作为公共财产——为 IO 移动服务进入边缘社区提供城市政策层面的合法性。
