— 00  /  The Project · 项目介绍
Background · 项目背景

NetNook 聚焦远程办公与城市可持续发展的结构性矛盾，将须于2033年前翻新的空置低效建筑转化为触手可及的社区共享办公场所。

NetNook is a service design initiative repurposing vacant buildings mandated for renovation under EU regulation, transforming idle urban spaces into accessible, near-home coworking environments.

Methodology · 设计方法

遵循双钻模型，以用户为中心展开桌面调研与实地访谈，精准绘制需求与痛点，为创新解决方案落地提供依据。

Following the Double Diamond model — desktop research and on-the-ground fieldwork through questionnaires and interviews precisely mapped user needs and systemic pain points.

Ecosystem · 服务生态

核心是连接五类利益相关者的完整生态——预订应用、模块化Hub、B2B合作与社区运营，以节能、归属感与可及性为价值内核。

A connected system linking five stakeholder groups — a booking app, a modular hub, B2B partnerships, and community programming — engineered to reduce energy consumption and bring coworking beyond the city centre.

不只是一个办公空间——而是以节能意识、社区连结与「好的工作应就在家附近」这一信念为基础的新型城市基础设施。

Not just a workspace — a new kind of urban infrastructure built on energy consciousness, community, and the belief that good work should happen close to home.

02 / The Scenario · 场景调研

我们从十年视角出发，分析各类变化趋势的价值走向，从而揭示了节能在本项目中的核心意义。这一研究过程让我们识别出以下六项关键洞察：

We explored a ten-year perspective by analysing the values of change, which revealed the need for energy conservation in our initiative. This process and research led us to identify these insights:

总体形势
General Situation

人们将为专业知识在城市间流动，形成越来越多的临时社群。网络将更分散、更广泛——非技能型工作者将更脆弱，贫富差距将持续扩大。

People move between cities for specialised knowledge, creating temporary communities. The wealth gap will grow.

出行与流动
Mobility

贫富差距与气温升高将促使人们迁往郊区，城郊、城际乃至跨省的通勤需求随之增加，火车与飞机将成为关键连接纽带。

Wealth gap and rising temperatures push people to suburbs; cross-city travel for work increases significantly.

住房与科技
Housing & Technology

城市基础设施老化，而技术以惊人速度迭代。AI与家居自动化的发展将远超建筑本身的改造速度——住宅将跟不上其内部科技的节奏。

Infrastructure ages while AI and home automation advance rapidly; buildings won't keep up with the technology they house.

从业者
Employees

灵活就业将成为常态。持续流动意味着关系不稳定、固定支撑点减少——人们对人际连结与归属感的渴望将日益强烈。

Flexible jobs become the norm; constant movement drives a growing need for human connection and belonging.

企业
Companies

企业将越来越多地提供弹性远程办公日，减少对大型总部的依赖。许多公司甚至不再设固定办公地点，但仍偶尔需要共享会议空间。

Companies increasingly offer smart-working; many will have no fixed location yet still need occasional shared spaces.

环境与能源
Environment & Energy

气候变化将日益主导日常生活——气温持续攀升推动制冷需求激增。在欠发达地区，能源匮乏将愈发严峻，即便那里的能源需求仍在增长。

Rising temperatures drive cooling demand; energy scarcity becomes critical in developing regions as needs grow.

02.2 / Background Research · 背景调研

价值变化的研究让我们得出结论：城市是本项目最具潜力的落地场景。NetNook 将战略重心聚焦于米兰，尤其是其充满活力的共享办公领域。

The identified values of change conclude that the city provides the most promising context. NetNook's strategic decision is to focus on the city of Milan, with emphasis on the dynamic realm of coworking.

气候变化加剧

气温持续攀升将成为日常生活中越来越重要的影响因素，制冷系统需求激增，极端天气与能源危机将愈发频繁。

More severe climate change — temperatures reaching higher peaks, increasing demand for cooling systems and dealing with weather crises.

米兰70%的住宅处于F、G能效等级

据ISTAT数据，米兰人口约135万，城市中约43,000栋住宅楼，70万套建于1919–1960年。仅0.6%的住宅达到A或A+级。

In Milan 70% of houses are class F & G — 43,000 blocks (66%), 700,000 dwellings built 1919–1960. Only 0.6% reach A or A+.

2050年城镇化持续深化

预计2050年意大利人口减少8%，城镇化率将升至81.8%，越来越多的人向城市聚集。

More urbanisation by 2050 — Italy's population projected to decrease by 8%; urbanisation rate reaches 81.8%.

须于2033年完成翻新，但并非所有业主都负担得起

法规要求住宅须从G级改造至F级（2030年截止）。至2033年，意大利310–370万套住宅（共1220万套）面临强制翻新。

Italians must renovate by 2033 — 3.1–3.7M of 12.2M residential properties need redevelopment; not every owner can afford it.

城心与郊区之间的巨大财富鸿沟

Brera-Moscova 年均工资 €94,554，Quarto Oggiaro 仅 €17,986，收入差距高达 €76,568。地理位置即决定命运。

Geographical disparities — city centre vs suburbs income gap of €76,568 (Brera-Moscova €94k vs Quarto Oggiaro €18k).

人们对家庭能耗普遍缺乏认知

消费者往往以使用频次来估算能源消耗，存在"认知可及性"偏差——越常想到的，越以为用得多，节能意识因此被扭曲。

People don't know their home energy use — consumers use frequency of interaction as a proxy for consumption; "cognitive accessibility" bias.

700万意大利人远程办公

超过700万意大利人从事远程工作，其中约27%在办公室与家庭之间交替，约15%完全居家办公。大城市居民远程比例明显高于小城镇。

7 million Italians work remotely — 27% alternating office & home, 15% fully remote. Larger cities show higher rates.

公司与员工之间的矛盾

90% 的企业表示将在2024年底前要求员工返回办公室；60% 的员工表示若被强制返岗将选择辞职。

Conflicts with company — 90% plan office return by end of 2024; 60% of employees would quit if forced back.

03 / The Service · 服务设计
NetNook Service

In this section, the NetNook service is introduced, emphasizing the concept of being part of a community with individuals from similar networks. The focus is on smart-working efficiently and comfortably, all while prioritizing energy conservation.

本节介绍 NetNook 服务理念：建立同频社群、高效舒适地智慧办公，同时将节能作为核心优先事项。

Vision

Saving energy, getting people closer.

节能减排，让人与人的距离更近。

Mission

NetNook is a smart-working service that allows workers to work closer to their home in an efficient environment if their homes are not efficient enough. It aims to create temporary communities of workers that can produce networking. It wants to give a second life to empty houses with a low level of energy efficiency.

NetNook 是一项智慧办公服务，让员工能够在距家更近、更高效的环境中工作——尤其适合那些家庭能效不足的人群。我们致力于在本地构建临时性工作社群，促进邻里之间的社交网络连接，同时为大量能效低下的空置房屋赋予第二次生命，实现空间的可持续利用。

关于NetNook的5个核心问题

The 5 Ws — a classic formula for information gathering

Who · 服务对象
NetNook 专为在非节能住宅中远程工作的职场人设计——高额能源账单与低效居家环境是他们面临的核心挑战。

Designed for remote workers in non-energy efficient homes. Ideal for professionals seeking a productive workspace close to home, creating temporary communities with local connections.

When · 使用时间
在标准工作时段内开放，同时支持课外活动预约。提供最短按小时预订与企业年度订阅两种模式，兼顾短期弹性与长期稳定需求。

Available during standard working hours with out-of-hours events possible. Flexible booking: minimum hourly for short-term needs; annual subscriptions for larger companies.

What · 服务内容
NetNook 将闲置住宅转化为设施完备的共享工作空间，构建本地小型办公室网络，取代大型集中式企业办公室，提供节能且高性价比的工作解决方案。

An innovative smart-working service transforming unused homes into fully equipped workspaces — a network of small local offices as an alternative to large, centralised corporate spaces.

Why · 存在意义
帮助企业去中心化，让员工在离家更近的小型办公室工作——减少通勤压力、降低个人能耗，促进社区连结，推动城市复兴与环境可持续。

To decentralise the workforce, reducing commuting stress and energy costs, encouraging community engagement and local networking — pioneering urban revitalisation and environmental sustainability.

Where · 空间布局
NetNook 的节点战略性地分布于城市各处，缩短员工出行时间，盘活闲置住宅，以便利可达性促进更好的工作与生活平衡。

NetNook's offices are strategically distributed in the city — reducing travel time, revitalising unused residential properties, and providing convenient access to promote a better work-life balance.

The Hub — A Workspace in Your Neighbourhood
Hub——就在你社区里的办公空间
A foldable, modular, energy-efficient coworking unit installed inside vacant apartments — designed to be light, sustainable and welcoming. / 可折叠的模块化节能共享办公单元，安装于空置公寓内——轻盈、可持续、温馨舒适。

NetNook's hub is a self-contained coworking environment that decreases the energy companies use — both directly in offices and indirectly from employees' homes during remote working.

The hub covers 3000mm × 3750mm on the ground with a height of 2500mm. Designed to be light, foldable and easy to install in any apartment room.

Hub是独立的共享办公环境，占地3000×3750毫米，高2500毫米，轻巧可折叠，可安装于任何公寓房间。

Structure · 结构
Aluminium Alloy 6061 — 40mm × 2mm tubes, foldable joints. Lightweight, rigid, compresses for transport.
铝合金6061，直径40mm，壁厚2mm，关节可折叠，收缩运输，展开承重。
Exterior Cover · 外覆材料
Tensotherm — 3-layer PTFE fiberglass: weather barrier + aerogel insulation + acoustic liner. Natural light, thermal & acoustic insulation.
Tensotherm三层结构：防水屏障、透光气凝胶隔热层、隔声内衬。透光隔热降噪。
Energy Infrastructure · 能源系统
EcoFlow Delta 2 (3000 cycle, 80% in 1h) + Wave 2 Portable Conditioner (14h, cools to 20°C in 5min, R290 refrigerant).
EcoFlow Delta 2储能站 + Wave 2便携空调，5分钟降温，14小时续航。

Final Considerations · 触点总结

The conducted survey and research proved that employees need another location apart from their homes and offices — combining the amenities of the office with the comfort of home, without compromising time lost on transportation. NetNook's flexible structure addresses this need directly. / 调研证明，员工需要一个兼具办公便利与居家舒适的第三空间，且无需牺牲通勤时间。NetNook的灵活结构直接回应了这一需求。

Sustainability · 可持续性

Infrastructure of energy systems in old buildings can be challenging. NetNook aims to decrease consumption by diminishing artificial light, providing "green" ventilation, and allowing house owners to generate income without spending energy on their estates.

旧建筑能源基础设施挑战大。Hub通过减少人工照明、绿色通风降低能耗，也让房东无需额外支出即可获得收益。

Flexibility · 灵活性

Different locations have different needs due to usage by different people. To cover as many needs as possible, very light framed modular furniture is used, integrable within the hub in various combinations.

不同地点、不同人群需求各异。轻型模块化家具可灵活组合，覆盖多种使用场景。

Mobility · 移动性

The light and shrinkable structure is aimed to be easily carried and installed on site. It is light enough to be carried by 2 people, flexible enough to install on site and can easily pass through doors.

轻巧可收缩的结构，两人即可搬运安装，可顺利通过门洞，快速部署至任意房间。

Composability · 可组合性

Designed to fit inside city apartment rooms, its dimensions match an average Milanese room (3×4 m). The structure is composed of identical modules that can be repeated to fit spaces with different dimensions.

针对米兰标准公寓房间尺寸设计（3×4米），模块可重复拼接，适应不同空间。

Material · 材料

The desired interior feeling is cosy and warm yet private and isolated. To achieve this, the hub uses wood flooring combined with special textiles on walls and roof — creating a contrast between the cold aluminium structure and warm fabric with wood.

内部追求温馨私密感：木质地板结合特殊织物墙体与顶面，在冷峻铝合金结构与温暖木布之间形成鲜明对比。
